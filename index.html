Privacy Policy – Harvest Hero

Effective date: 1 November 2026

In short: the game itself does not collect, upload or share any personal data. It has no ads and no analytics. On Android, you sign in with your HUAWEI ID, which Huawei handles.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for or collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services and plays fully offline.

HUAWEI ID sign-in (Android)

The Android app uses HUAWEI Game Service (part of HMS Core) so that it can be listed in HUAWEI Game Center. When the game starts, it asks you to sign in with your HUAWEI ID. Huawei handles the sign-in. It gives the game your HUAWEI ID nickname, profile picture and an anonymous player identifier. The game does not store or upload this information or send it anywhere. The HUAWEI SDK needs network access and some related Android permissions for this. Huawei processes your HUAWEI ID data under Huawei's privacy statement. You can cancel the sign-in and still play the whole game. The web version has no sign-in.

Game progress

Your farm progress and settings are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. They never leave your device and are not included in Android cloud backups. They are deleted when you choose Settings → Reset Save, uninstall the game, or clear its data.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone.

In-app purchases

There are none. The game is free and has no purchases or gambling mechanics.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, this page will be updated and the effective date changed.

Dasar Privasi – Harvest Hero

Ringkasnya: permainan ini sendiri tidak mengumpul, memuat naik atau berkongsi sebarang data peribadi. Tiada iklan dan tiada analitik.

Di Android, permainan meminta anda log masuk dengan HUAWEI ID melalui HUAWEI Game Service supaya ia boleh disenaraikan dalam HUAWEI Game Center. Log masuk dikendalikan oleh Huawei. Permainan menerima nama samaran, gambar profil dan pengenal pemain tanpa nama, tetapi tidak menyimpan, memuat naik atau menghantar maklumat ini ke mana-mana. Anda boleh membatalkan log masuk dan masih bermain sepenuhnya. Versi web tiada log masuk.

Kemajuan dan tetapan disimpan hanya dalam peranti anda, tidak termasuk dalam sandaran awan Android, dan dipadam apabila anda memilih Tetapan → Padam Simpanan, menyahpasang aplikasi atau mengosongkan data aplikasi. Tiada pembelian dalam aplikasi. Sesuai untuk semua peringkat umur.
